A contour cannot start from a vague image. Thresholding makes a decision for every pixel: foreground or background. Connected foreground pixels become one candidate object.

32–41 MIN · BREAK THE RULE

WHEN DOES
ONE BECOME TWO?

Contours count connected regions, not real-world intentions. If two objects touch, their boundaries can merge and the system may count one region instead of two.

CONTOUR OR
DEEP DETECTOR?

Use contours when objects are simple, visible, and separate. Use a trained detector when real objects vary in texture, scale, background, viewpoint, or class.
